Latest Update

Update 1.0.4 — Reactive Seven-Kanda RPG Foundation

The latest update number is 1.0.4. The familiar Ramayana Adventure layout now contains a living RPG foundation beneath the existing story card.

Major additions:

  • Seven-kanda atlas covering Bala, Ayodhya, Aranya, Kishkindha, Sundara, Yuddha, and Uttara Kanda with canonical anchors and reactive play opportunities.
  • Hidden world-state model for dharma, knowledge, courage, wisdom, devotion, persuasion, stealth, agility, endurance, craft, perception, community reputation, relationships, discoveries, quests, time, weather, and player history.
  • Visible Your Journey panel that records meaningful actions without revealing every hidden consequence.
  • Reusable contextual mini-game framework notes for memory, trivia, rhythm, observation, dialogue, navigation, pattern, reconstruction, timing, sorting, language, map reading, resource management, meditation, crafting, music, and logic.
  • Action-based progression buttons for market mediation, manuscript study, forest scouting, and rhythm practice.
  • Python WorldStateEngine prototype for deterministic condition checks, relationship memory, reputation-ready state, and unlock evaluation.
